## Evergreen post bank
1. "Which is it?" Post a `scene` + question from any pack as a text card and give the answer in the comments the next day.
2. **Trap of the week.** One wrong answer that most people pick, and why it is wrong.
3. **Ten-second games.** Screen recordings of each solo game, with no voiceover and the caption as the instructions.
4. **Projector cam.** Real class footage with faces blurred or from behind: the reveal moment.
5. **Upload your quiz** tutorial: spreadsheet, then Save as CSV, then Upload, then it's in your party.
6. **Teacher testimonial** (once you have them): one quote, the teacher's subject, nothing else.
7. **Leaderboard** of solo high scores people send you.
